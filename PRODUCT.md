# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People encountering Yearn vault links in social feeds, chat, search, and other
link-preview surfaces. They need to recognize the product, understand its
headline yield and scale, and trust that the preview belongs to Yearn.

## Product Purpose

Generate accurate, recognizable social preview cards for Yearn products. Each
card should preserve the product's own visual identity while making the most
important live vault metrics legible at thumbnail size.

## Brand Personality

Confident, geometric, and direct. Product variants should feel unmistakably
Yearn while retaining the strongest visual cues of their underlying ecosystem.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS cards, decorative gradients, shadows, glass effects,
excessive uppercase text, and dense dashboard-like layouts. Product-specific
cards should not overwrite the visual identity of unrelated vault cards.

## Design Principles

- Make the product recognizable before the metrics are read
- Prioritize one truthful headline metric with clear context
- Preserve Yearn identity while acknowledging product-specific ecosystems
- Use flat composition, strong spacing, and contrast that survives thumbnails
- Keep visual variants isolated so the default card remains stable

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain strong text contrast and large type, and never rely on color alone to
communicate a metric. Keep labels explicit so the meaning survives resizing,
compression, and low-resolution previews.
